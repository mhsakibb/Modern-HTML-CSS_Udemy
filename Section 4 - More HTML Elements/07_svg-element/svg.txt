SVG (Scalable Vector Graphics)
================================
XML-based vector image format for 2 dimensional
graphics with support for interactivity and animation.

Can be created and edited with any text editor as well
as graphic software.

SVGs do not lose quality when resized or zoomed in.

Ex:
---
<svg width="100" height="100">
  <circle cx="50" cy="50" r="40" fill="red" />
</svg>
